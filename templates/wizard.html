Welcome

Let's get your TikTok MC Integrator set up. Takes about a minute.

TikTok account

The TikTok username whose LIVE stream the bot connects to. No password needed — it reads your public live events.

Minecraft server (RCON)

RCON lets the bot send commands to your server. Enable enable-rcon=true in server.properties and set a password there.

Spotify (optional)

Enable song requests and queue control. You can connect your Spotify account from the dashboard after setup — this just turns the feature on.

✅

All set!
